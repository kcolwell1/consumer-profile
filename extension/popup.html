Connect your orbit.

With your approval, Orbit reads visited URLs, page titles and visit times for your selected period. Data is sent only to your open dashboard at kcolwell1.github.io/consumer-profile/ and held in memory.

No passwords, cookies, tokens, private browsing, or account access.

Up to 3,000 URLs / 20,000 visits per analysis. Sensitive topics remain unclassified in the dashboard. No data is saved by this extension.
